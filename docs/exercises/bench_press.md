## Setup tips shown before starting (not checked by the camera)

- Eyes under the bar, head neutral (bp_01).
- Shoulder blades squeezed together and down, chest up (bp_02).
- Bar low in the palm, wrists straight, thumbs around the bar (bp_05, bp_06).
- Feet flat on the floor, about shoulder-width (bp_04).
- Elbows about 75° from the torso, not flared to 90° (bp_12, SL). Not checked: needs a camera above you.

## Where sources disagree (for the coach to decide)

- **bp_03 arch:** SL: natural arch, no overarch; TB: natural arch; BWS: individual; FV: competition-style larger arch
- **bp_12 elbow_angle:** SL: about 75 deg; BWS: individual; affects chest region emphasis; FV: natural; avoid too low
- **bp_14 chest_contact_point:** SL: mid-chest / sternum; TB: lower chest

## Common errors named by the sources

| Error | Do instead | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbless ('suicide') grip | Wrap thumbs around the bar | SL, TB |
| Elbows flared 90 deg | Tuck about 75 deg | SL |
| Bent wrists | Grip low in palm / bulldog grip | SL |
| Smith machine bench | Free weights in a rack | SL |
| Benching without rack/spotter | Use power rack with pins | SL |
| Lowering the bar straight from the uprights to chest | Unrack and balance over shoulders first | SL |
| Not locking elbows | Lock at the top of every rep | SL |
| Flat chest / no arch | Raise chest, natural arch | SL |
| Butt off bench | Keep butt down; check bench height | SL, TB |
| Feet in the air or on the bench | Feet flat on floor | SL, TB |
| Bouncing the bar off the chest | Touch and go, brush chest | SL |
| Half reps | Touch the chest | SL |
| Pushing the head into the bench | Keep neck neutral | SL |
| Holding breath poorly / no breathing plan | Follow brace-and-hold plan | SL |

## Limitations

- Numbers come from coaching texts, not biomechanics measurements; most limits are [Placeholder] values to be set from labeled reps and a coach (docs/how-scoring-works.md).
- Angles come from a 3D skeleton: the 2D landmarks plus the person's segment lengths (body profile, or learned in the first seconds), with MediaPipe's depth used only for toward/away. This lets side-view checks run from the front or at 45°, but those views are not yet validated against a side camera (docs/body-and-3d.md). Without world landmarks the app falls back to 2D angles from the side nearer the camera.
- The pose model has no spine points: rounding or arching of the back can't be measured.
- There is no bar keypoint; the hands stand in for the bar.

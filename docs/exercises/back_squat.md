## Camera

Rep detection: knee angle; at rest it is high; a rep starts past 150°, ends back past 160°, and only counts if it reaches 120°. [Placeholder] thresholds.

## When a movement counts as a rep

A movement is counted when at least 80% of these pass (criteria that can't be measured are left out). Otherwise the app says which ones failed. [Placeholder] values.

- knees bent to 120° or less
- hips dropped by 15% of leg length
- both knees bent
- hips bent
- took 0.6–10 s
- feet stayed in place
- stance unchanged

## Setup tips shown before starting (not checked by the camera)

- Heels about shoulder-width, toes out about 30° (sq_01, sq_02).
- Bar high on the traps or low across the rear delts, centered (sq_04).
- Squeeze the shoulder blades and raise the chest before unracking (sq_07).
- Big breath at the top, hold it on the way down (SL).
- Safety pins just below your bottom position (sq_10).

## Where sources disagree (for the coach to decide)

- **sq_19 depth:** SL: hip crease strictly below top of knees; LIFT: thighs at least parallel; DYN: parallel or just below parallel
- **sq_21 bottom_pause:** SL: no pause; DYN: pause briefly

## Common errors named by the sources

| Error | Do instead | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Partial / half squats | Break parallel (hip crease below top of knees) | SL |
| Knees cave inward | Push knees out in line with feet on way down and up | SL |
| Heels come off floor | Stance shoulder-width, toes 30 deg out, knees out; no plates under heels; hard-soled shoes | SL |
| Leaning forward / hips rise faster than chest (good-morning) | Raise hips and chest together, keep back angle constant | SL |
| Lower back rounding | Knees/toes out, do not go deeper than parallel-ish, natural arch | SL |
| Narrow stance so belly blocks depth | Heels shoulder-width, toes out 30 deg | SL |
| Very wide sumo stance | Heels about shoulder-width | SL |
| Looking up / using mirrors | Neutral neck, film yourself instead | SL |
| Supporting the bar with the hands/wrists | Carry it on the upper-back shelf | SL |
| Bar off-centre | Center before unracking | SL |
| Unracking with feet behind the bar or lunge-style | Feet under bar, stand straight up | SL |
| Rounding the back while squatting | Keep spine neutral, chest up | BOS |
| Squatting in the Smith machine / box squat as crutch / leg press as replacement | Free-weight squat in a power rack | SL |

## Limitations

- Numbers come from coaching texts, not biomechanics measurements; most limits are [Placeholder] values to be set from labeled reps and a coach (docs/how-scoring-works.md).
- Angles come from a 3D skeleton: the 2D landmarks plus the person's segment lengths (body profile, or learned in the first seconds), with MediaPipe's depth used only for toward/away. This lets side-view checks run from the front or at 45°, but those views are not yet validated against a side camera (docs/body-and-3d.md). Without world landmarks the app falls back to 2D angles from the side nearer the camera.
- The pose model has no spine points: rounding or arching of the back can't be measured.
- There is no bar keypoint; the hands stand in for the bar.

## Setup tips shown before starting (not checked by the camera)

- Start with feet hip- to shoulder-width (lu_01).
- Step long enough for a 90° front knee (lu_02).
- Land heel first, front heel stays down (lu_03).
- Front and back knee point straight forward (lu_07).

## Where sources disagree (for the coach to decide)

- **lu_01 start_stance:** PE: hip-width; CV: shoulder-width
- **lu_06 position:** TG: under the hip; CV: hover; no touch; LC: light brief touch allowed

## Common errors named by the sources

| Error | Do instead | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Front knee collapses inward | Keep knee tracking over the foot | NS, PE, TG, EX, CV |
| Stride too short | Step longer than a walking stride | NS |
| Over-striding | Step 1-1.5 foot lengths; land on the heel | CV |
| Forward torso lean | Stay upright, engage core | NS, EX, CV |
| Front knee passes toes | Keep it over the ankle | CV |
| Too much pressure on the front of the foot | Press through the heel | PE |
| Rotating the back knee/foot outward | Point rear foot straight forward | NS |

## Limitations

- Numbers come from coaching texts, not biomechanics measurements; most limits are [Placeholder] values to be set from labeled reps and a coach (docs/how-scoring-works.md).
- Angles come from a 3D skeleton: the 2D landmarks plus the person's segment lengths (body profile, or learned in the first seconds), with MediaPipe's depth used only for toward/away. This lets side-view checks run from the front or at 45°, but those views are not yet validated against a side camera (docs/body-and-3d.md). Without world landmarks the app falls back to 2D angles from the side nearer the camera.
- The pose model has no spine points: rounding or arching of the back can't be measured.
- There is no bar keypoint; the hands stand in for the bar.

## Setup tips shown before starting (not checked by the camera)

- Feet hip-width, bar over the middle of the foot (dl_01, dl_02).
- Grip about hip-width, arms straight and vertical (dl_03).
- Brace before the pull: breath in, get tight (dl_07).
- Neutral spine the whole lift (dl_06). Not checked: the camera has no spine points.
- Lower by pushing the hips back first, bend the knees once the bar passes them (dl_11).

## Where sources disagree (for the coach to decide)

- **dl_04 shoulder_position:** SL: shoulder blades over bar; MT: shoulder blades over bar; HT: shoulder blades over bar; SU: bar aligned with posterior deltoid; MC: shoulders above hips; over hips at lockout

## Common errors named by the sources

| Error | Do instead | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Lifting with a rounded / unstable spine | Neutral spine for the whole lift | MT, SL |
| Hips shoot up first, back rounds | Get tight before the pull | SU |
| Bar too far in front of mid-foot | Bar over mid-foot | SU, SL |
| Bending at the back instead of hinging at the hips | Push butt back, break at hips | GR |
| Lifting by extending the back rather than the hips | Extend hips; glutes and hamstrings are prime movers | GR |
| Bouncing the weight off the floor | Dead-stop each rep | MT |
| Leaning back at lockout | Finish tall | MT |
| Letting ego choose the weight | Prioritize form | MT |

## Limitations

- Numbers come from coaching texts, not biomechanics measurements; most limits are [Placeholder] values to be set from labeled reps and a coach (docs/how-scoring-works.md).
- Angles come from a 3D skeleton: the 2D landmarks plus the person's segment lengths (body profile, or learned in the first seconds), with MediaPipe's depth used only for toward/away. This lets side-view checks run from the front or at 45°, but those views are not yet validated against a side camera (docs/body-and-3d.md). Without world landmarks the app falls back to 2D angles from the side nearer the camera.
- The pose model has no spine points: rounding or arching of the back can't be measured.
- There is no bar keypoint; the hands stand in for the bar.

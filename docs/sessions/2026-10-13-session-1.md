# Session 1 — 2026-10-13, first real test (front view, phone, Lite model)

9 reps detected. The app flagged every one. Analysis with `analysis/analyze_session.py` (the exported JSON is kept out of the repo: personal data).

## Why good reps were flagged

| Cause | Reps affected | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration done while not centered/facing the camera (`shift0 = −0.42`: hips sat well to one side of the feet). Every later rep was measured as "shifted" by ~45–50% | 1–3, 5, 6 | Reject calibration unless hips are over the ankles and both feet are level; normalize shift by hip width |
| User or phone moved after calibrating: ankles 16–22% lower in the frame, so depth and heel baselines were stale | 3, 5, 6 | Detect that the feet moved and ask to recalibrate automatically |
| Tracking glitches counted as reps (1–19 frames, points off-screen, left/right ankles swapped) | 4, 7, 8, 9 | Drop reps with bad tracking instead of scoring them |
| Depth target on the edge: 99% reached → "shallow" | 6 | Tolerance band, and validate the depth proxy with a side view |

Reps 3, 5 and 6 were, by the measurable signals, good: deep (99–117% of target), knees well out (1.8–2.4× ankle width), heels down, slow descent (3–4.7 s), small real shift (0.06–0.08 of hip width).

## Angle check

The same knee angle at the bottom read 77° vs 69° (left vs right) in 2D and 70° vs 97° using MediaPipe's depth estimate. Knee and hip bend angles are not usable from the front view.

The export didn't record the frame size, so x/y were converted assuming a 3:4 portrait frame (estimated from shoulder width vs torso length). The database schema now stores `frame_width`/`frame_height`.
